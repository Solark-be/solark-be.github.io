02 — Legacy & modernity

From critical existing systems to agentic AI

More than twenty years running, integrating and selling critical systems, from long-established platforms to hybrid clouds. Since 2024, agentic AI has changed the game: what seemed frozen can be documented, rewritten, connected and automated — as long as a human remains accountable at every step.

A

Understand what exists

AI-assisted reverse documentation of applications and infrastructure: RPG or COBOL code, scripts, configurations, business rules.

B

Modernise step by step

Rewrite, migrate or wrap: keep what works, replace what holds you back, without a big bang.

C

Orchestrate and automate

Agent chains to deploy, operate and report: speed, restart capability and traceability, from infrastructure to applications.

D

Steer without waiting for migration

Connect data from existing systems (ERP, CRM, mailboxes) to make decisions before everything is merged: merger, consolidation or transition.

Run a programme, not a project

Compared scenarios, business plan, skills and hiring plan, governance and milestones: I initiated and led a modernisation programme for critical legacy environments within an IT integrator, through to executive approval.

03 — Public sector

Supporting public bodies, from assessment to contract award

A public IT project is often won or lost in the tender specifications. Solark helps contracting authorities express the right need, choose the appropriate procedure under Belgian public procurement rules and compare bids on solid grounds — then stays at their side during implementation. Step by step or end to end.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Analysis of the current situation, departmental needs and budget or grant constraints.

  2. 02

    Draft

    Tender specifications: technical requirements, selection and award criteria, bill of quantities.

  3. 03

    Award

    Choice of procedure, bidder questions, negotiation, bid evaluation and award recommendation.

  4. 04

    Support

    Coordination and monitoring of contractors during installation, through to acceptance.

Example assignment

Digital transformation of a Walloon municipality

Project supported by the Walloon Region (“Tax on Pylons” call for projects): fibre-optic links between municipal sites, replacement of physical servers with a cloud solution and a move to IP telephony.
